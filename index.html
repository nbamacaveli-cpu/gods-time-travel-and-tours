Cabin Classes Explained

Economy Class: Usually the most affordable cabin option.

Premium Economy: Available on selected airlines, sometimes with additional space and comfort.

Business Class: May provide premium seating, meals and additional services.

First Class: Available on selected airlines and aircraft with premium seating and services.

Aircraft layouts and cabin features vary by airline, route and aircraft. Always confirm your flight details before booking.
