Aircraft and Cabin Experiences

Learn about aircraft types and the different cabin experiences available on selected flights.

Economy Class

Usually the more affordable cabin, with standard seating and services depending on the airline and ticket.

Premium Economy

Available on selected airlines and aircraft, sometimes offering more legroom and additional amenities.

Business Class

May offer larger seats, premium meals, lounge access when eligible and lie-flat seats on selected routes.

First Class

Offered by selected airlines, sometimes with private suites, premium dining and personalised services.

Aircraft, cabin layouts, baggage allowances and onboard services vary by flight. Always confirm the details with the airline before booking.
