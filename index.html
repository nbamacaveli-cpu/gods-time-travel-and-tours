Cabin classes explained

Economy Class
Usually the most affordable cabin, with standard passenger seating and services determined by the airline and ticket.

Premium Economy
Available on selected airlines and aircraft. It may offer extra legroom, a wider seat or additional services.

Business Class
May offer larger seats, lie-flat beds on selected aircraft, premium meals and lounge access when eligible.

First Class
Offered by selected airlines, with premium seating or suites and additional services on equipped flights.

Cabin classes, seat layouts, baggage rules and onboard amenities differ. Confirm details for your exact flight before booking.
