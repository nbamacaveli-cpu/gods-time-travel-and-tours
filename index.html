Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Does this website issue flight tickets?

No. It prepares your enquiry in an email. A booking is not made until confirmed with the relevant provider.

Can you guarantee a visa?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority.

Are the package amounts flight prices?

No. They are enquiry-package options. Airline fares, government fees and other costs must be confirmed separately.

Are documents uploaded automatically?

No. This version does not upload or attach files. Confirm the correct process before sending sensitive documents.
